COMMUNITY RULEBOOK

CHHAT
CRICKET RULES

Every rooftop has chaos. These rules simply help record it properly.

1

Unlimited Overs Match

No over restriction unless agreed manually.

2

Toss Decides First Pick

Winning side selects the first player.

3

Alternate Player Selection

Teams draft players one by one alternately.

4

Every Ball Counts

Deliveries are tracked even in unlimited matches.

5

Career Stats Are Permanent

Match performances automatically update player records.

6

Boundaries Only In Straight

Side hits do not count as boundaries.

7

Off Side Wall Is Out

Hitting the off side wall directly results in dismissal.

8

No Running Between Wickets

Runs are earned only through boundaries.

9

Only 4s And 6s Count

No singles, doubles or extra running runs.

10

Catch Must Stick Cleanly

The ball must stay in control immediately after the catch.

Play Fair. Hit Hard.

Rooftop cricket was never meant to be calm.
